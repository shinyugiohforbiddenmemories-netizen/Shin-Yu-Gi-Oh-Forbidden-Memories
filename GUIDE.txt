- In “Password,” the Starship cost is reduced to half, plus an additional 5 drops per duel.

The core idea consists of a minimalist enhancement of the game, correcting specific issues from the original version without altering its core identity.

All 722 cards will be available in the game, either through the improved fusion system from Pocket Station or through a FREE DUEL 2 mode featuring more unlockable characters, where cards that were originally obtainable via IR can now be acquired by dueling 39 new characters. The game's standard droplist will remain largely unchanged, with only very minor adjustments. The fusion system will be rebalanced by removing certain specific cards, with very few exceptions, in order to improve overall balance and internal consistency. Furthermore, three specific cards will be rebalanced:

The following cards will no longer be obtainable as FREE DUEL drops before completing the main campaign through the normal progression:

 - Meteor B. Dragon

 - Blue-Eyes Ultimate Dragon

 - Gate Guardian

Drops change:

By default, each character drops 10 cards instead of 1. This 'does not increase the overall drop rate', as all probabilities are already calculated assuming a total of 10 drops. 

In practice, most drops are regular cards, while a small portion may become special cards. The entire drop system is balanced with these 10 drops in mind, meaning the probability of obtaining rare cards is already fully accounted for within the default drop calculation.

The drop rate for obtaining at least 1 card out of the 10 drops is standardized by rarity. The available rarities are:

P(≥1 in 10 trials):

1. Common:		   ?
2. Rare:		15,0%
3. Super Rare:		 8,0%
4. Ultra Rare:		 4,0%
5. Secret Rare:		 1,5%

Common cards share the remaining probability equally among all possible non-rare cards. The distribution always follows the rarity order from the most common to the rarest available cards.


Obs:			P(p) := P(≥1 in 10 drops) = "P" Probability of at least 1 drop per win in function of "p" probability per drop
	 		P(p) = 1-(1-p)^10, where p is the prob of 1 card
	Consequently	P(p<<1) = 10p.

Expected N (<N>) is the average number of trials required to observe the m drops. Since the actual number of trials is random, the result may be higher or lower than this value.

The standard deviation (σ) measures this variation. A result within Expected N ± 1σ is common, while larger deviations (±2σ, ±3σ, etc.) become increasingly unlikely. The table below can be used to estimate how unusual it is to observe the event after a given number of trials.

Notes:

Three distinct dragon lineages:

White Holy Line:
The simplest and most powerful lineage. It requires relatively few fusion steps, but offers only a one fusion paths.

Black Dragon Line:
A very powerful lineage with multiple evolution routes. It provides access to some of the strongest dragon fusions and several advanced forms.

Darkness Dragon Line:
Less powerful than the White and Black lineages, but highly versatile. It features numerous fusion possibilities, making it the most flexible path to develop.
